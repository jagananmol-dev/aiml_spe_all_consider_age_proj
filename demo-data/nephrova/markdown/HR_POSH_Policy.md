# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy

Nephrova Renal Care Pvt. Ltd. has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, as required by the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013.

## Internal Committee

- Presiding officer: Anjali Thomas, Chief Nursing Officer
- Members: Snehal Patil (HR Manager), one senior nurse, and one external member from a women's welfare organisation

## Making a complaint

- Write to the Internal Committee at ic@nephrova.example within 3 months of the incident.
- The committee completes its inquiry within 90 days and keeps the complaint confidential.
- No one who complains or gives evidence in good faith will face any adverse action.
